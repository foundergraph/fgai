02 — The problem

Inbound is a queue of admin.

A pitch arrives as an email, a forwarded deck, a form response. Then someone copies it into the deal sheet, sends the confirmation, chases the call. The work is not the judgment — it is the handoffs.

03 — The pipeline

One pipeline. Every pitch.

From application to scheduled call, in the same system your team already uses. No spreadsheets, no email threads, no dropped requests.

04 — Application experiences

Any front door. One pipeline.

Plain form

The full application on a single page.

Typeform-style

One question at a time, with a progress bar.

Agent-led

The founder answers in a conversation.

Any of the three can sit on your fund's site — each feeds the same deal pipeline.

05 — The workflow

What runs, step by step.

  1. 01

    Capture

    One structured application: company, stage, MRR, ask, traction and deck.

  2. 02

    Log

    A row lands in the shared deal sheet; the founder is confirmed and the team notified.

  3. 03

    Decide

    A partner sets Status to Interested — the only manual action.

  4. 04

    Engage

    The founder is emailed a booking link and picks a slot.

  5. 05

    Track

    The meeting time is written back to the same row.
